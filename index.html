Credit Transfers

Instructions (Click to expand)

  • Pick your major from the colourful dropdown at the top of the sidebar. You'll see that as you choose a different option that the arrangement of subjects in the main grid changes.

    You'll see that the 3 options are:

    • Network Security
    • Business Analytics
    • Software Development

    Your 1st Semester? You don't need to decide yet (assuming you don't have credit transfers either).

    • You can just leave the dropdown as Undecided or switch it to another.
    • Some soft advice that will help keep your options for next semester and beyond:
      • Choose BIT121 if you have a feeling that Network Security may become your major.
      • Study BIT105 and BIT111 this semester.
      • If you have a feeling Software Development may become your major, study BIT111 now.

    Your 2nd semester? By second semester, you should be starting to settle on your choice for major. If unsure, try dipping your toe into 1 or 2 streams.

    Your 2nd year? If you have passes or credits for 8 subjects or more, you should now be certain about which major stream will be yours. Your goal now is to complete 3 of your major's subjects, but preferably 5 of them, by the end of your 2nd year.

  • Enter your passes and credits.

    There are two easy ways:

    • Clicking mode: Click the "Clicking Mode" button in the sidebar to go into History Mode. You can then click subjects in the grid to mark them completed.
    • Typing mode: Open a panel by clicking the "By Typing" button. Then paste or type in subject codes (e.g. BIT111, USE101). If you provide a block of text, any subjects codes within will be extraced

    Only enter your completed subjects - passes and credit transfers. That is, don't enter failed subjects or withdrawals.

    You'll see that when you add a subject, the main the grid and sidebar change:

    • Its subject card greys-out.
    • Subjects that are available to you now, to study this semester, changes. You'll see them highlighted with background shadows in the main grid, and in the sidebar list. Note that all 2nd and 3rd year level subjects have prerequisites

  • Choose your timetable: Select subjects in one of these ways.

    1. Click subjects in the main grid.
    2. Click a subject in the sidebar under Select.
    3. Open the Select panel and click subjects there.

  • When you have selected your full semester load, a new button will appear: "Your semester plan".

    Click it to open a panel that shows your timetable. Copy that table and email it to us at enrol_bit@melbournepolytechnic.edu.au.

    How many subjects will you study this semester?
    Explanations:

    Domestic students can study full-time or part-time (so, 1, 2, 3, or 4 subjects) each semester.

    International students must normally study a full load of 4 subjects (visa requirement). This can only be varied in exceptional circumstances such as a high failure rate that led to an academic intervention.

    More the 4 subjects? Studying more than 4 subjects is only possible when 9 or fewer subjects remain in the final year or 5 or fewer subjects remain in the final semester AND a CR average (which is 60 to 69%) has been achieved over the past year.
