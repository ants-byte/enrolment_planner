Instructions

Quick guide: Use this web site to record completed subjects (your passes and credit transfers if you have any), see a list of subjects that you can study this semester, and then build your timetable.

  1. Step 1: Pick your major from the dropdown at the top of the sidebar.

    By second semester, choose a stream: Network Security, Business Analytics, or Software Development and give some focus to completing subjects in that stream. Changing stream after that may delay graduation.

    In first semester, it’s okay to leave this as Undecided. The planner uses Network Security as a placeholder only - you can switch next semester.

  2. Step 2: Enter your passes and credits.

    There are two easy ways:

    • Clicking mode: Click subjects in the grid to mark them completed.
    • Typing mode: Paste or type subject codes (e.g. BIT111, USE101). Extra text, spaces, or subject names don’t matter—the codes are pulled out automatically.

    Completed subjects will darken in the grid, and your available subjects will update based on prerequisites.

  3. Step 3: Choose your timetable by selecting subjects. Three ways:

    1. Click subjects in the main grid.
    2. Click a subject in the sidebar under Select.
    3. Open the Select panel and click subjects there.

    Set study load

    Domestic students can study 1, 2, 3, or 4 subjects each semester.

    International students must normally study a full load of 4 subjects (visa requirement). This can only be varied in exceptional circumstances such as a high failure rate that led to an academic intervention.

    Studying more than 4 subjects is only possible when 9 or fewer subjects remain in the final year or 5 or fewer subjects remain in the last semester.
